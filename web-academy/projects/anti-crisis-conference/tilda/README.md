## Smoke test after publishing

- Free mode: personal fields stay hidden and disabled; the CTA opens BotHelp;
  close it by button, overlay, and Escape, and verify keyboard focus returns.
- Paid RU: enter name, email, and a valid Russian number, then open the cart.
  Verify exactly one 1 999 RUB product, matching name/email, and one +7 prefix.
- Reopen the paid cart: quantity must remain one; no duplicate product is added.
- Paid international: test Belarus or Uzbekistan. The cart must retain the full
  +375 or +998 E.164 number. Kazakhstan shares +7, so verify all national digits.
- Invalid or incomplete phone values must remain on the local form with an
  inline error. Tinkoff and Dolyame must remain selectable in the native cart.
- Check widths 375, 768, 1024, and 1440 px, keyboard navigation, the country
  search field, console errors, failed requests, and horizontal overflow.
- Stop before entering or charging a real card.
